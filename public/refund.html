Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Currently free

Hook Studio is free to use today. There are no payments, subscriptions, or paid tiers, so there is nothing to refund right now.

If paid features launch

We may introduce paid features in the future, such as a subscription or one-time credit packs. If we do, the following general terms will apply unless a specific offer says otherwise:

  • Subscriptions: you can cancel anytime; cancelling stops future billing but doesn't refund the time already paid for in the current billing period, except where the law gives you a right to a refund (see below).
  • One-time purchases (e.g. credit packs): refundable if unused and requested within a reasonable period (e.g. 14 days), except where a purchase has already been substantially used.
  • EU/EEA consumers: if you're a consumer in the EU/EEA, you generally have a 14-day right to withdraw from an online purchase of digital services, unless you've expressly asked for the service to start immediately and acknowledged that you lose this right once it's fully performed.

This page will be updated with exact pricing and refund terms once a specific paid feature actually launches.
