Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Short version

Hook Studio does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. We don't run Google Analytics, ad pixels, or any similar service, and we don't store anything in your browser's local storage.

Why there's no cookie banner

Cookie consent banners are required when a site uses non-essential cookies (tracking, analytics, advertising, etc.). Since Hook Studio doesn't use any of those, we haven't added a banner. If that changes in the future — for example, if we add privacy-friendly analytics — we'll update this policy first and add a consent banner if the cookies we introduce require one.

Technical cookies from our hosting provider

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may occasionally set strictly necessary technical cookies (for example, for security or bot protection) as part of delivering the site reliably. These are exempt from consent requirements under GDPR/ePrivacy because they're required for the service to function and aren't used to track you across sites or build an advertising profile.
