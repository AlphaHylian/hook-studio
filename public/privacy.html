Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Who we are

Hook Studio is a small, independent tool operated by AlphaHylian. You can reach us at ayandabose2021@gmail.com with any questions about this policy or your data.

What we collect

Manual mode runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or anyone else.

Automatic mode (beta) sends the video concept, goal, platform, and hook count you enter to our server, which forwards them to a third-party AI provider (currently OpenRouter and whichever model it routes your request to) solely to generate hooks for you. We do not store this text ourselves, do not link it to your identity, and do not use it for anything other than producing that one response. Please don't enter personal or sensitive information into automatic mode.

We don't require an account, login, or payment details to use Hook Studio today. If paid features are introduced later, this policy will be updated to describe any additional data that involves (such as billing information handled by a payment processor).

Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers on this site. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Automatic-mode text is processed by OpenRouter and the underlying AI model it routes to, only to generate your hooks. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Data retention

Since we don't log or store automatic-mode requests ourselves, there is generally nothing on our end to retain. Our hosting and API providers may keep standard technical logs for a limited time under their own policies.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, GDPR gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to object to processing. Because we don't retain personal data tied to you, most requests will simply confirm we hold nothing about you. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for Estonia, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon). Residents of other regions may have similar rights under their local law.

Children's privacy

Hook Studio is not directed at children and isn't intended for use by anyone under 13 (or the digital consent age in your country, if higher).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
